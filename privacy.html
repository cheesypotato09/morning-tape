Privacy PolicyMorning Tape. Last updated September 22, 2026.

Morning Tape is a free daily markets quiz game run by an Indiana University student. This page explains what information the game collects and how it's used.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, the game receives your name and email address. It also stores your answers, scores and the times you played each round, plus any display name you choose.

How it's used

Your email is used only to identify your account. Your display name and scores appear on the game's leaderboard, which other signed-in players can see. Nothing is used for advertising.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your information. Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the game's database, and sign-in is handled by Google.

Deleting your data

To have your account and scores removed, email [YOUR EMAIL] and they'll be deleted.
